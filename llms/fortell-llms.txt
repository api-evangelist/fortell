# Fortell

> AI-powered hearing aids clinically proven to deliver superior speech clarity in noisy environments. Built from the ground up to amplify conversation without amplifying background noise.

Fortell is a hearing technology company that has developed revolutionary AI hearing aids using proprietary spatial-AI algorithms and custom low-latency silicon to solve the "Cocktail Party Problem" – the difficulty of understanding speech in noisy social settings. Founded by Matt de Jonge (CEO), Andrew Casper (CTO), Cole Morris (COO), and Igor Lovchinsky (Chief Scientific Officer), Fortell has raised $150M in funding and launched its first clinic on Manhattan's Upper East Side.

## Core Technology

Fortell's breakthrough technology combines:
- **Custom Silicon Chip:** Proprietary chip processes audio in under 10 milliseconds to preserve natural sound
- **Spatial AI:** Advanced source-separation AI identifies and isolates voices directed at the wearer
- **Real-Time Processing:** Pulls conversation into the foreground while attenuating background noise
- **Deep Neural Networks:** Trained on massive synthetic datasets emulating diverse acoustic conditions

Unlike traditional hearing aids that simply amplify all sounds equally, Fortell changes what you hear – not just how loud everything is.

## Clinical Validation

- Randomized controlled trials co-designed and overseen by outside academics
- All participants showed materially improved speech comprehension in background noise
- 9.2 dB improvement over leading competitors in clinical testing
- 18.9X higher odds of understanding speech versus top AI hearing aids
- 95% of wearers preferred Fortell over top-of-the-line competitors in blind comparisons

## Optional: Extended Information

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a prescription?**
Yes. Fortell AI Hearing Aids are for prescription use by adults only. You'll need either a hearing exam in the Fortell clinic or an audiogram from the past 6 months.

**Who are these hearing aids for?**
Adults with mild to profound hearing loss. A consultation with an audiologist is required to confirm candidacy.

**What is the return policy?**
100-day trial period. If they're not right for you, return for a full refund.

**How long does the battery last?**
18+ hours even with Fortell AI on its most aggressive setting. The charging case holds an additional 60 hours of charge.

**Can I connect to my phone?**
Yes. Fortell AI Hearing Aids connect to iPhones via Bluetooth for streaming calls, music, and podcasts. The companion app provides controls and care team access.

**What warranty is included?**
3-year comprehensive warranty plus 3 years of loss and damage coverage. Full details at fortell.com/warranty.

**How much do they cost?**
Contact Fortell or visit the NYC clinic for current pricing. The purchase includes the device pair, accessories, 5 years of audiological care, warranty, and loss/damage protection.
